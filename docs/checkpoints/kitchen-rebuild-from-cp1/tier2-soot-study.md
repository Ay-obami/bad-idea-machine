This is a transparent 180×120 supplemental soot texture, generated with the built-in image tool from the approved photograph's cream-tile and warm-light reference. The first generated version contained replacement tile pixels and was rejected. The accepted-for-testing cutout contains only dark soot residue and alpha; it is laid over the existing backsplash at x=390, y=175 with 0.28 opacity. It does not replace a cabinet, tile rectangle or any master pixels. Tier 1 stationary pan, toast and ceramic placements are reused for context. The soot has its own hide/show control and is absent from intact and Tier 1 modes.

The cutout adds 7,310 bytes to the Kitchen rebuild art; the budget changes from 14 assets / 435,000 bytes to 15 assets / 445,000 bytes. The authored-room and full-bundle limits remain unchanged. The cutout decodes as transparent WebP. This is a local visual study, **not a completed Tier 2 terminal composition**. Live inspection must verify that the soot looks attached to the tile and burner origin, has no pasted edge, and disappears cleanly when hidden. Cabinet damage, surface chips, broken parts, contacts and any motion remain outside this study.
